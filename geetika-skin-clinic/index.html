How a visit works

Four clear steps.

You know what is planned, and why, before any treatment starts.

  1. Consultation

    Tell the doctor what you have noticed. Your skin, scalp or hair is examined and your history noted.

  2. A plan, explained

    Options are explained in plain words: what is involved, how many sessions are advised and side effects to know about.

  3. Treatment

    Procedures are carried out at the clinic, with aftercare instructions to follow at home.

  4. Review

    Follow-up visits, as advised, to check how your skin is responding and adjust the plan.

Questions

Before you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call 99151 67078 or 0172-4646476, or send a WhatsApp enquiry, to check a suitable time. Clinic timings: [clinic timings].

What happens at the first consultation?

The doctor asks about your concern and medical history, examines the skin, scalp or hair, and explains the options. If a test is needed first, you are told why.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on the concern and the treatment. The number of sessions and the gap between them are advised after the consultation and may be adjusted at review visits.

Is laser hair reduction suitable for me?

Suitability depends on your skin type, hair colour and medical history, so it is checked at a consultation before any session is booked.
