Skin

Acne & acne scars

Acne is a common skin condition in which blocked pores and inflammation cause blackheads, whiteheads, red spots and sometimes deeper, painful lumps. Marks and scars can remain after it settles. Care starts with an examination to understand the type of acne, then may combine skin-care advice, prescribed medicines and in-clinic procedures. The clinic lists acne and scar therapy among its treatments.

Sessions

Acne care usually runs over weeks to months, with review visits as advised. If scar procedures are suitable, they are planned as a series, also as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Use only the products and medicines prescribed for you.
  • Avoid picking or squeezing spots; it can deepen marks.
  • Use a gentle cleanser and a daily sunscreen.
  • Keep your review appointments so the plan can be adjusted.
Skin

Pigmentation

Pigmentation means darker patches, spots or uneven tone. Common triggers include sun exposure, hormonal changes and marks left after acne or irritation. The type of pigmentation is identified first, because it guides the choice of care. Options the clinic lists include chemical peels and laser treatments, used alongside home care.

Sessions

Number of sessions as advised. Pigmentation often needs a combination of daily home care and several clinic sessions spaced some weeks apart.

Aftercare (general)

  • Wear a broad-spectrum sunscreen every day and reapply outdoors.
  • Avoid harsh scrubs and products not advised by the doctor.
  • Tell the clinic if your skin becomes irritated or darker.
Skin

Chemical peels

A chemical peel is a solution applied to the skin for a set time to loosen and remove the outer layer of dead cells. The clinic's current website describes peels as helping in removing dead skin, lightening pigmentation and improving overall texture. The type and strength of peel are chosen after your skin is assessed.

Sessions

Usually a series of sessions spaced a few weeks apart; the number is as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Mild redness, tightness or flaking for a few days is common.
  • Let flakes come away on their own; do not peel them.
  • Moisturise, and use sunscreen every day.
  • Avoid waxing, scrubs and other active products on the area until the doctor says it is fine.
Hair

Laser hair reduction

Laser hair reduction uses light energy that targets the pigment in hair follicles to reduce regrowth over a course of sessions. Settings depend on your skin and hair type, which is why suitability is checked first. The clinic and its directory listings list laser hair removal among its services.

Sessions

Several sessions are needed because hair grows in cycles; the number and the gap between sessions are as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning before and after sessions.
  • Do not wax, thread or pluck between sessions unless advised.
  • Mild redness or warmth usually settles within hours.
  • Use sunscreen on treated areas that are exposed.
Hair

Hair loss & PRP

Hair thinning or shedding can have many causes, such as genetics, stress, nutrition, hormones or a scalp condition, so the cause is evaluated first. Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) is one option the clinic lists: a small sample of your own blood is processed to concentrate the platelets, which are then injected into the scalp. The clinic's current website describes PRP for androgenetic alopecia (pattern hair loss) in men and women. Hair transplantation is listed under cosmetic surgery.

Sessions

The clinic's current website describes a typical hair-PRP course of 3–6 sessions, with maintenance sessions every 4–6 months. Your own plan is as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Mild tenderness of the scalp for a day or so is common.
  • Wait for the time advised before washing your hair.
  • Avoid heavy exercise, heat and hair colouring on the day, or as advised.
Anti-ageing

Anti-ageing injectables & lifts

Anti-wrinkle (botulinum toxin) injections relax specific muscles that form expression lines. Dermal fillers are gels injected to restore volume or contour. Thread lifts use fine dissolvable threads placed under the skin to lift and support. The clinic lists Botox, dermal fillers, thread lifts and skin tightening among its treatments.

Sessions

Effects of these treatments are temporary, and repeat treatment, if wanted, is as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Do not rub or massage the treated area on the day.
  • After anti-wrinkle injections, stay upright for the hours advised.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas and alcohol for the time advised.
  • Small bruises or swelling can appear and usually fade.
Skin

HydraFacial & microneedling

HydraFacial is a machine-based facial that cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates the skin. Microneedling uses very fine needles to create tiny channels in the skin; combined with PRP it is sometimes called a “vampire facial”. The clinic's website describes both treatments.

Sessions

HydraFacial can be a single visit or repeated periodically. Microneedling is usually planned as a series. Both as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Redness for a day or two after microneedling is common.
  • Skip make-up for the period advised.
  • Use gentle products and a daily sunscreen.
Medical

Vitiligo, rosacea & psoriasis

These are long-term skin conditions that are diagnosed and managed medically. Vitiligo causes patches of skin to lose colour; rosacea causes facial redness, flushing and bumps; psoriasis causes thickened, scaly patches. The clinic lists all three among the conditions it treats. A plan is made after diagnosis and reviewed over time.

Sessions

Ongoing care with regular review visits, as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Use medicines exactly as prescribed.
  • Note anything that seems to trigger a flare.
  • Do not stop or change treatment without speaking to the doctor.
Procedures

Warts, corns & dermabrasion

Warts are growths caused by a viral infection of the skin; corns are areas of thickened skin caused by pressure or friction. Both can be removed in the clinic, with the method chosen after examination. Dermabrasion is a controlled resurfacing of the upper layers of the skin. Directory listings for the clinic include wart removal, corn removal and dermabrasion.

Sessions

Some are done in one sitting; others need a few, as advised.

Aftercare (general)

  • Keep the area clean and dry, and dress it as advised.
  • Contact the clinic if you notice increasing pain, redness or discharge.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this information a treatment plan?

No. It is general information to help you prepare questions. Your own plan, including sessions and aftercare, comes from the doctor after a consultation.

Can treatments be combined?

Sometimes. Whether treatments can be combined, and in what order, is decided by the doctor after examining your skin.

What should I tell the doctor before a procedure?

Mention any medicines or supplements you take, allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding, recent sun exposure or tanning, and any past reactions to skin treatments.
