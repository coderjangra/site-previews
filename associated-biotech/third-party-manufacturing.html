The process

Seven steps, one point of contact.

Every product goes through the same sequence. Knowing it up front means fewer surprises between enquiry and dispatch.

  1. Step 01

    Enquiry

    Send your company name, the dosage form and segment you are interested in, an approximate quantity and the state you will market in. The form on this page puts all of it into one email.

    You share

    Company details, dosage form, segment, rough quantity, target state.

    We handle

    A reply by email with the next steps [confirm usual response time].

  2. Step 02

    Product & composition selection

    Pick products from the current list or share the composition you want. Strength, pack size and pack type are fixed at this stage, so the rest of the process runs on agreed details.

    You share

    Composition and strength, pack size, pack type.

    We handle

    Feasibility check against the product list and a quotation.

  3. Step 03

    Documentation & approvals

    Third-party manufacturing needs paperwork on both sides before production can start. The documents depend on the product and your state, and the list is shared at this step.

    You share

    Your company and licence documents [confirm document list].

    We handle

    The manufacturing-side permissions for your product [confirm process].

  4. Step 04

    Artwork & packaging

    Your brand name and artwork go on the strip or bottle label and the carton. Proofs are checked against the agreed composition and label text before anything is printed.

    You share

    Brand name, logo and artwork, or a design brief.

    We handle

    Proofs for your approval [confirm artwork support].

  5. Step 05

    Production

    Once documents, artwork and packing material are in place, the batch is scheduled and manufactured at the plant.

    You share

    Final approval of artwork and the order.

    We handle

    Manufacturing at the plant: [confirm plant address].

  6. Step 06

    QC release

    Batches are tested by quality control and released before they are dispatched.

    You share

    Any specific test or document requirement, up front.

    We handle

    QC testing and release [confirm documents shared with each batch].

  7. Step 07

    Dispatch

    Released stock is packed for transport and dispatched to the address you give in your target state.

    You share

    Delivery address and transport preference.

    We handle

    Packing and dispatch [confirm dispatch terms].

FAQ

Questions partners usually ask.

What is third-party pharma manufacturing?

A marketing company sells medicines under its own brand name while a licensed manufacturer makes them. The manufacturer produces, tests and packs the product; the marketing company handles sales and distribution.

Which dosage forms can you manufacture?

Tablets, capsules, dry syrups and injectables. Ask for the current product list in the dosage form you need.

Which segments does the range cover?

Beta-lactam, neuro & joint pain, cardiac & diabetic, proton-pump inhibitors (PPIs) and nutraceuticals, with herbal and cosmetic products [confirm range].
