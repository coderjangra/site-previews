About the company

A Panchkula pharma manufacturer, incorporated in 2005.

Associated Biotech Private Limited manufactures tablets, capsules, dry syrups and injectables on a third-party and contract basis for pharma marketing companies and PCD franchise owners.

01 — Company record

The details partners check first.

Associated Biotech was incorporated in Haryana in 2005 and has its registered office in Sector 8, Panchkula. It works as a third-party and contract manufacturer: marketing companies own the brand and the market, and the products are made, tested and packed by Associated Biotech.

02 — How we work

Three things every partner can expect.

Agreed before it is made

Composition, strength, pack and artwork are agreed with the partner before a batch is scheduled.

Checked before it ships

Batches are tested and released by quality control before they are dispatched.

Clear on paper

The documentation for third-party manufacturing is worked through with each partner, product by product.
