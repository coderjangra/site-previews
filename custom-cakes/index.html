What the shop needs to know

Six details make a clear order.

The order builder asks for each of these, so nothing gets missed when your message lands.

  • Flavour

    Chocolate, vanilla, butterscotch, or your own favourite.

  • Size

    The weight you have in mind, or how many people it’s for.

  • Eggless or with egg

    Say your preference up front.

  • Message on top

    Names, ages, wishes, spelt exactly how you want them.

  • Theme & colours

    A colour, a character, or a reference photo in the chat.

  • Date & pickup

    When you need it, and whether you’ll pick it up or need delivery.

How do I place a custom cake order?

Use the order builder: choose the occasion, flavour, size, egg preference, the message on top and your date. It opens WhatsApp with the whole order typed out, ready to send to Custom Cakes. You can also call +91 79860 61054 or +91 98763 69191.

Can I ask for an eggless cake?

Choose “Eggless” in the builder, or mention it when you call. The shop will confirm what’s possible for your flavour and design.
