Cakes & occasions

Cakes for every kind of celebration.

From a first birthday to a farewell at work, tell Custom Cakes what you’re celebrating and how you picture it. Here are a few occasions to get you started, and if yours isn’t here, it still counts.

Build your cake order

Occasions

Birthdays

From first birthdays to big milestones: a name and an age on top, favourite colours, or a theme the birthday person loves.

Order a birthday cake
Good to mention
  • Name and age for the message
  • Theme or favourite colours
  • Roughly how many guests

Anniversaries

Mark the years together with something sweet, whether that’s simple and elegant or full of hearts.

Order an anniversary cake
Good to mention
  • Both names, or the year
  • Colour preference
  • The date you need it

Baby showers & naming

Soft colours, little stars and the baby’s name if it’s been chosen, for a godh bharai, baby shower or naming day.

Order a baby shower cake
Good to mention
  • Theme colours
  • The name, if it’s ready
  • Eggless or with egg

Weddings & engagements

For a roka, engagement, sangeet or the wedding itself. Share the colours of the day and the guest count.

Order a wedding cake
Good to mention
  • Function and date
  • Colour palette
  • Number of guests

Kids’ parties

Cartoons, superheroes, unicorns, dinosaurs, cricket: bring the theme and a reference picture if you have one.

Order a kids’ party cake
Good to mention
  • Character or theme
  • A reference photo
  • Message for the birthday kid

Office & get-togethers

Farewells, promotions, team wins and festival get-togethers at work or at home.

Order an office cake
Good to mention
  • Company or team name
  • Number of people
  • Pickup or delivery

Your choices

Every detail, your call.

These are the choices the order builder walks you through, one step at a time.

  • Flavour

    Pick a favourite or describe your own. The shop will confirm what’s available.

  • Weight

    Half a kilo, a kilo, two, or tell us how many people it’s for.

  • Shape

    Round, square, heart or tiered, or let the shop suggest.

  • Eggless or with egg

    Say your preference up front so it’s part of the order.

  • Message

    Up to 40 characters, spelt exactly as you want it on the cake.

  • Design & theme

    Colours, a character, a theme. Share reference photos in the chat.
