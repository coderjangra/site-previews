Pregnancy care

Expecting? Here's how your visits usually flow.

A simple outline of antenatal care. Your own schedule is set at your visits, depending on your health and your baby's.

Read the pregnancy care guide

  1. As soon as you know

    First visit

    Confirming the pregnancy, your health history and an estimated due date.

  2. Weeks 1–13

    First trimester

    Early check-ups, with blood tests and a scan as advised.

  3. Weeks 14–27

    Second trimester

    Regular checks of blood pressure, weight and your baby's growth.

  4. Weeks 28–40

    Third trimester

    More frequent visits, your baby's movements and your birth plan.

Questions

Before your visit

Short answers to common questions. For anything about your own health, please ask the doctor directly.

Do I need an appointment for the Sector 7 clinic?

Yes. Ashirwad Gynae Clinic is a residence clinic that works by appointment on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 6:00–7:30 PM. Please WhatsApp or call 98771 81151 first.

Where does Dr. Shilva see patients during the day?

At Cloudnine Hospital, Panchkula (Mansa Devi Complex, Swastik Vihar, Sector 5, MDC), Monday to Saturday until 4:00 PM. [confirm hospital OPD timings]

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any earlier reports, scans and prescriptions, the date of your last period, and a list of the medicines or supplements you take.

Can I book through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button or the enquiry form on the contact page. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready to send to 98771 81151.

Is the information on this site medical advice?

No. It is general information to help you prepare. Your care is always planned with the doctor at your visit. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call 112.
