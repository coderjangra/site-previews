About

About Dr. Shilva

Obstetrician and gynaecologist in Panchkula, trained at PGIMER Chandigarh, with an evening residence clinic in Sector 7.

[photo of Dr. Shilva]

Background

Training and experience

Dr. Shilva completed her MBBS at Government Medical College, Patiala, and her MD in Obstetrics & Gynaecology at PGIMER, Chandigarh, where she went on to work as a Senior Resident.

She has worked as a Specialist Medical Officer with the Haryana Civil Medical Services (HCMS), as Head of the Department of Obstetrics & Gynaecology at Dhawan Hospital, Panchkula, and as Chief Consultant, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, at Paras Bliss Hospital. She is now a senior gynaecologist at Cloudnine Hospital, Panchkula (formerly Paras Bliss Hospital), and sees patients in the evenings at Ashirwad Gynae Clinic, her residence clinic in Sector 7.
