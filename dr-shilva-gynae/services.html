Gynaecology & pregnancy services

An overview of what Dr. Shilva helps with at Ashirwad Gynae Clinic, Sector 7, and at Cloudnine Hospital, Panchkula. The details of your care are always discussed with you at your visit.

  • Pregnancy care
  • Delivery
  • Infertility evaluation
  • Gynaecology consultations
  • Cervical cancer (HPV) vaccine
  • Periods & menopause

Pregnancy care

Regular antenatal check-ups help keep track of your health and your baby's growth in each trimester. Dr. Shilva's listed areas of experience include antenatal care and high-risk pregnancies.

A visit may include

  • Confirming the pregnancy and estimating your due date
  • Blood pressure, weight and general check-ups
  • Blood tests and ultrasound scans, as advised
  • Guidance on diet, activity and supplements

Good to know

  • It helps to book as soon as you know you are pregnant, or when you are planning a pregnancy
  • Bring earlier reports and any pregnancy file you have
  • See the pregnancy care guide for what to expect

Delivery

Planning for birth starts well before your due date. Dr. Shilva discusses delivery with you during antenatal visits, including normal delivery and the pain-relief options available during labour.

A visit may include

  • Talking through your birth preferences
  • What to do when labour starts, and when to come in
  • Pain-relief options during labour
  • What happens if a different plan becomes safer for you or your baby

Good to know

  • Ask early which hospital your delivery is planned at [confirm delivery hospital]
  • Keep your reports together in one file for the hospital
  • Pack a hospital bag in the last weeks of pregnancy

Infertility evaluation

If you have been trying for a baby without success, an infertility evaluation looks at possible reasons for both partners. When treatment is needed, the options are explained step by step, including IUI where it is suitable.

A visit may include

  • A detailed history of cycles, earlier pregnancies and health
  • Examination and ultrasound, as advised
  • Tests for both partners, as advised
  • An explanation of options such as IUI

Good to know

  • Come together for the first visit if you can
  • Bring all earlier test reports and prescriptions
  • Every couple is different; the doctor will explain what your results mean for you

Gynaecology consultations

Consultations for common gynaecological concerns, from routine check-ups to symptoms that need a closer look.

A visit may include

  • A conversation about your symptoms and history
  • An examination, explained to you first
  • Ultrasound or tests, as advised
  • A clear plan and follow-up

Good to know

  • You can ask for anything to be explained before it is done
  • Bring a list of your current medicines
  • Write down your questions before the visit

Cervical cancer (HPV) vaccine

The HPV vaccine helps protect against the types of human papillomavirus most often linked to cervical cancer. Dr. Shilva's site notes that it is routinely recommended for girls aged 11 or 12, can be given from age 9, and does not protect against every type of HPV.

A visit may include

  • Checking age and suitability
  • Explaining the dose schedule
  • Answering questions for parents and young women
  • Advice on regular cervical screening for adults

Good to know

  • The vaccine does not replace cervical screening
  • Ask about availability before your visit [confirm vaccine availability]

Periods & menopause

Support for period concerns, such as irregular, heavy or painful periods, and for the physical and emotional changes around menopause. Menopause-related concerns are among Dr. Shilva's listed areas of experience.

A visit may include

  • A history of your cycles and symptoms
  • Tests, as advised
  • Lifestyle guidance
  • Treatment options explained, so you can decide together

Good to know

  • A simple period diary for two or three months helps
  • Mention hot flushes, sleep or mood changes, even if they seem minor

This page describes services in general terms. It does not promise any particular result; what is right for you is decided with the doctor after an examination.
