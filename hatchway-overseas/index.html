Questions

Before you book.

Quick answers to what students usually ask first. Anything else, just call or send a WhatsApp.

Call +91 62391 15622

Which courses does Hatchway Overseas offer?

Coaching for IELTS (Academic and General Training), PTE Academic and the Duolingo English Test, French courses for DELF A1, A2, B1, B2, TCF and TEF, and spoken English classes.

What are the class timings?

IELTS and PTE batches, and the Smart PTE Lab, run between 7 AM and 9 PM, and weekend batches are available. For current French, Duolingo and spoken English slots, message us on WhatsApp or call +91 62391 15622.

IELTS, PTE or Duolingo: which test should I take?

It depends on what your university, employer or visa route accepts and the score it asks for. Check their requirements first, then use a demo class to see how each test works. Our quick comparison sets out the basic differences.

What is the Smart PTE Lab?

It is the practice lab at our Phase 6 centre for PTE students. PTE Academic is taken on a computer with a headset, so lab practice gets you used to working on screen. Ask at the centre how lab sessions fit around your batch.

Where is the centre?

SCF-32, 1st Floor, above Bajaj, Phase 6, Sector 56, Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar (Mohali), Punjab 160055. See directions and the map.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the course and the batch you choose. Call +91 62391 15622 or send a WhatsApp message for current fees.
