Four skills

What every English test asks of you.

IELTS, PTE and the Duolingo English Test all check the same four skills, just in different formats.

Listening

Following different accents, catching details and keeping pace with the recording.

Reading

Skimming, scanning and managing your time across long passages.

Writing

Planning and structuring a clear answer within a time limit.

Speaking

Speaking fluently and on topic, to an examiner or into a microphone.
