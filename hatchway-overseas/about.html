About us

A hatchway is a way through.

Hatchway Overseas is a PTE institute coaching students for IELTS, Academic and spoken English proficiency, from a first-floor centre in Phase 6, Mohali.

The name

An opening you step through.

A hatch is an opening, a way from one space into the next. For many students in Mohali, an English or French test is exactly that: the step between a plan to study or work abroad and actually going.

Hatchway Overseas exists to prepare you for that step. Under one roof in Phase 6 you will find coaching for IELTS, PTE Academic and the Duolingo English Test, French courses from DELF A1 to B2 with TCF and TEF preparation, and spoken English classes.

IELTS and PTE batches run from 7 AM to 9 PM, there are weekend batches, and PTE students practise in the Smart PTE Lab.
