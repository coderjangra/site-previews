The courses

What each course covers.

Each test checks the same four skills in a different way. Here is what you will be preparing for.

IELTS

IELTS

For university study abroad (Academic) or work and migration (General Training).

Book an IELTS demo

What it covers

  • Listening: following recordings and answering as you go
  • Reading: long passages under time pressure
  • Writing: two timed tasks (report or letter, and an essay)
  • Speaking: a face-to-face conversation with an examiner

Format

Four skills, each given a band score from 0 to 9, plus an overall band. You can sit IELTS on paper or on a computer.

Batches

Batches between 7 AM and 9 PM. Weekend batches available.

Course length and fees: ask us when you book your demo.

PTE

PTE Academic

A fully computer-based test, scored automatically. Practise in our Smart PTE Lab.

Book a PTE demo

What it covers

  • Speaking & Writing: answering into a microphone and typing responses
  • Reading: on-screen reading tasks
  • Listening: audio and video clips with short tasks
  • Lab practice on a computer with a headset

Format

Taken on a computer at a test centre in about two hours. Scores run from 10 to 90.

Batches

Batches and Smart PTE Lab between 7 AM and 9 PM. Weekend batches available.

Course length and fees: ask us when you book your demo.

DET

Duolingo English Test

A short online English test you take on your own computer.

Book a Duolingo demo

What it covers

  • Reading, writing, listening and speaking through short question types
  • Questions that adapt to your level as you go
  • Timed writing and speaking responses
  • Getting your set-up right for an at-home test

Format

Taken online from home in about an hour. Scores run from 10 to 160.

Batches

Ask us for the current batch timings.

Course length and fees: ask us when you book your demo.

FRA

French

French from beginner level up, with preparation for recognised French exams.

Book a French demo

What it covers

  • DELF A1, A2, B1 and B2
  • TCF preparation
  • TEF preparation
  • Listening, reading, writing and speaking in French

Format

DELF levels follow the European A1 to B2 scale. TCF and TEF are French proficiency tests (both have versions used for Canadian immigration).

Batches

Ask us for the current batch timings.

Course length and fees: ask us when you book your demo.

ENG

Spoken English

For students, job-seekers and anyone getting ready to study, work or live abroad.

Book a spoken English demo

What it covers

  • Everyday conversation
  • Pronunciation and clarity
  • Vocabulary for college and work
  • Interview and presentation practice

Format

Practical speaking practice with no exam at the end, just more confident English.
