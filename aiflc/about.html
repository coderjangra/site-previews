Our story

AIFLC — also written A.I.F.L.C, and called the AIFLC Group on some of its pages — is a foreign language school, translation service and study-abroad guide at SCF 19, Sector 8-B, Chandigarh, near Domino's.

People learn a language for many reasons: an exam such as DELF, Goethe or DELE, a university place abroad, a visa interview, a job, or simple curiosity. AIFLC keeps classes, translation and guidance together, so one conversation covers all of it.

The school serves learners from Chandigarh, Panchkula and Mohali, and offers hostel / PG facilities for students coming from further away.
