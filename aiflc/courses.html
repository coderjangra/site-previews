FAQ

Before you enrol

Which level should I start at?

If you have never studied the language, start as a complete beginner (A1). If you have studied before, tell us what you covered and we will suggest a level.

Which exams can I prepare for?

French: DELF A1–B2, TCF and TEF for Canada. German: Goethe A1–B2. Spanish: DELE A1–B1. English: IELTS.

What are the batch timings and fees?

They depend on the language, the level and the course length. Arabic batches start every Monday. For everything else, call +91 98880 12118 or WhatsApp us for the current schedule. [batch timings]

Is accommodation available for out-station students?

Yes — hostel / PG facilities are available for both male and female candidates.

Not sure which language to pick?

Tell AIFLC why you are learning — an exam, a university abroad, a job, or curiosity — and get a suggestion.
