FAQ

Questions, answered

Which languages does AIFLC teach?

More than 15 foreign languages: Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, French, German, Dutch, Norwegian, Danish, Swedish, Finnish, Czech, Bulgarian, Russian, Greek, Polish, Arabic, Farsi, Chinese and Japanese — plus Hindi, Punjabi and Urdu for NRIs and foreign learners. See the Courses page for the full list.

How long is a course?

Courses run for 3 weeks, 6 weeks, 3 months or 18 weeks. Tell us your deadline — an exam, a visa interview, a move abroad — and we will help you choose.

Do you prepare students for DELF, Goethe or DELE?

Yes. French: DELF A1–B2, plus TCF and TEF for Canada. German: Goethe exam preparation A1–B2. Spanish: DELE A1–B1. IELTS preparation is also available for students planning to study abroad.

Can I learn online?

Online classes are offered for French, German and Spanish. [confirm current online batches]

I am coming from outside Chandigarh. Is there accommodation?

Yes — hostel / PG facilities are available for both male and female candidates. Ask us about current options when you enquire.

What are the fees and batch timings?

They depend on the language, the level and the course length. Call +91 98880 12118 or WhatsApp us for the current schedule and fees. [batch timings]
