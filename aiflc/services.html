How guidance works

Four steps before you fly

  1. Talk to us

    About the country, the course you want and your timeline.

  2. Prepare the language

    IELTS, or French, German or Spanish exams if your programme needs them.

  3. Get documents ready

    Including translations of certificates and records.

  4. Practise the interview

    Embassy interview coaching before your appointment.
