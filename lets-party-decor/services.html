Ideas to discuss

Details worth deciding early

Bring your thoughts on these to the first conversation. It makes planning quicker for everyone.

  • Theme & colours

    A palette or theme ties the whole look together. Even two or three colours are a great start.

  • Stage & backdrop

    Where the photos happen: the backdrop behind the couple, the cake or the speakers.

  • Entrance

    The first thing guests see, and a welcome that sets the mood.

  • Florals

    Marigolds, roses or something else? Share the flowers and colours you love.

  • Lighting

    Fairy lights, lanterns or candles change how a venue feels after dark.

  • Tables & seating

    Centrepieces, cake tables and seating areas for family and guests.
