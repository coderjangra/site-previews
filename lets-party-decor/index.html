Questions

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and ask directly.

Which areas does Let's Party Decor cover?

Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. The office is in Industrial Area Phase 2, Chandigarh.

What kind of events can I enquire about?

Weddings and parties, for example engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers and corporate events. If your occasion is not listed, just mention it in your enquiry.

How do I send an enquiry?

Use the Plan Your Event page: choose your occasion, add the date, venue, guest count and theme ideas, and the form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to send. You can also call +91 84278 33083 or +91 89687 00022, or email letspartydecor01@gmail.com.

How early should I get in touch?

As soon as you have a date, or even a rough month. Sharing it early gives you time to check availability and plan the decor together.

Can I share inspiration pictures?

Yes. Once the WhatsApp chat is open, attach any photos, colour palettes or screenshots that show the look you like.

How much does the decor cost?

It depends on the occasion, the venue, the guest count and the decor you choose. Share your details and discuss the options and costs directly with the team.
