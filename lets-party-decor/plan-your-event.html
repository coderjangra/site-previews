About the form

Before you send

Rather talk? Call +91 84278 33083 or +91 89687 00022.

Is anything sent automatically?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your message ready. You can read it, edit it and then press send yourself.

My date is not fixed yet. Can I still enquire?

Yes. Leave the date empty and mention a rough month or season in your theme ideas.

Can I share inspiration photos?

Yes. Once the WhatsApp chat is open, attach photos, screenshots or colour palettes you like.

Is my event outside the Tricity?

The listed service area is Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. If your venue is elsewhere, mention it in your enquiry.
