Before you pay

A short checklist.

General points worth asking about on any deal. This is not legal advice.

Is the project RERA registered?

For new projects, ask for the RERA registration number and check it on the Punjab RERA website.

Who holds the title, and is it clear?

Ask to see the ownership papers and check that the seller is the person on the title, with no pending loans or disputes.

What exactly is included in the price?

Ask about parking, maintenance, transfer charges and any other costs on top of the quoted price.
