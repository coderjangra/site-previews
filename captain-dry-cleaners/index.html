Care desk

Before you hand over your clothes.

A few small things that help your garments come back looking their best.

Empty the pockets

Pens, coins and tissues can mark the garment, and others with it.

Point out stains

Say what it is and how old it is. Try not to rub or wash it first.

Keep the care label

It tells us the fabric and the right way to clean it.

Mention delicate work

Beads, sequins, embroidery or loose buttons need extra attention.
