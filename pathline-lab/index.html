Before your test

A little preparation goes a long way.

General guidance only. Always follow your doctor’s instructions. See the full preparation guide.

  • Fasting tests

    Fasting blood sugar and lipid tests usually need an overnight fast of about 8–12 hours. Plain water is generally fine.

  • Your medicines

    Don’t skip a medicine on your own. Ask your doctor, and tell the lab what you take.

  • Urine samples

    A first-morning, midstream sample in the lab’s sterile container is usually preferred.

  • What to bring

    Your doctor’s prescription, if you have one, and any previous reports for comparison.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Only for some tests. Fasting blood sugar and the lipid profile usually need an overnight fast of 8–12 hours; many others, such as a CBC, thyroid profile or HbA1c, usually don’t. Check the notes on the Book a Test page and confirm when you book.
