Preparation guide
Before you give a sample.


General guidance only. Always follow your doctor’s instructions and confirm preparation with the lab when you book.


  • Fasting tests
    Usually 8–12 hours with nothing but plain water, as advised for your test. Book an early-morning slot so you don’t fast longer than needed. Examples: fasting blood sugar, lipid profile.
  • Medicines
    Keep taking your medicines unless your doctor says otherwise, and tell the lab what you take. Some, such as thyroid tablets or blood thinners, matter for certain tests.
  • Urine sample
    First-morning sample if possible. Clean the area, let the first part go, then collect the midstream into the lab’s sterile container and close it tightly.
  • Stool sample
    Collect a small amount in the clean container from the lab, without mixing urine or water, and bring it in fresh.
  • Sputum sample
    Early morning, before eating. Rinse your mouth with plain water, then cough deeply to bring up sputum from the chest. Saliva isn’t suitable.
  • Semen analysis
    Usually 2–7 days of abstinence. Collect the full sample in the lab’s sterile container and bring it in quickly; the lab will tell you the time limit.
  • Culture tests
    Ideally give the sample before starting antibiotics, if your doctor agrees. Culture reports take longer because organisms need time to grow.
  • Blood tests in general
    Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily, drink water unless told otherwise, and sit quietly for a few minutes before the sample.
  • What to bring
    Your doctor’s prescription if you have one, previous reports for comparison, and a list of the medicines you take.










Booking questions
About your request.


Is this a confirmed appointment?
No. The form sends a booking request. The lab replies to confirm the time, preparation and rate.
I can’t find my test in the list.
Type its name in the “Other tests” box, or send a photo of your prescription on WhatsApp after your message.
Can I book for a family member?
Yes. Put their name in the form and mention any details the lab should know, such as age or fasting status.
Which tests should I take?
Please follow your doctor’s advice on which tests you need. The lab can help with preparation and booking.
