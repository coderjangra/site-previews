Your first consultation

A plain guide to a first homoeopathic visit: how to prepare, what to bring, what happens in the room and how follow-ups work. It is general information; the doctor will guide you on anything specific to you.

Step one

Before you come

Get in touch to confirm timings and a suitable time. Then, if you can, spend ten minutes jotting down a few notes. Homoeopathic case-taking goes into detail, and notes make it easier to remember everything.

Things worth noting:

  • When it started
  • What makes it better
  • What makes it worse
  • Time of day it’s worst
  • Sleep
  • Appetite & thirst
  • Feeling hot or cold
  • Stress & mood
  • Recent life events

In the consultation room

During the consultation

There are no wrong answers. Some questions may seem unrelated to your main concern; homoeopaths ask them because they consider the whole person.

  1. 01

    Your concerns, in your own words

    You will be asked to describe what brings you in, how it started and how it affects your day. Take your time.

  2. 02

    Questions about you as a whole

    Expect questions about sleep, appetite, thirst, energy, mood, preferences for heat or cold, and how you react to stress. They help a homoeopath understand you as an individual.

  3. 03

    Your history

    Past illnesses, operations, family history and any treatment you are having now. This is where your reports and medicine list help.

  4. 04

    Reports and examination where needed

    The doctor may look through your papers and, where relevant, examine you.

  5. 05

    Your prescription and instructions

    You will be told how and when to take your medicine, what to note down, and when to return.

At home

After the visit

  • Take your medicine exactly as instructed, and keep the instructions somewhere easy to find.
  • Keep a short diary of any changes: sleep, energy, mood and your main concern.
  • Continue medicines prescribed by your other doctors unless they advise otherwise.
  • Contact the clinic if you are unsure about how to take your medicine.

If symptoms are severe or getting worse quickly, see a doctor or go to the nearest hospital straight away. In an emergency, call 112.

How long does a first consultation take?

A first homoeopathic consultation is usually longer than a follow-up, because it includes a detailed history. Please ask the clinic how much time to set aside: [typical duration]

Should I stop my current medicines?

No. Please don’t stop or change any medicine prescribed by another doctor unless that doctor advises it. Bring a list of everything you take, so Dr. Sachdeva has the full picture.

Why am I asked about sleep, appetite or mood?

Homoeopathic case-taking considers the whole person, not only the main complaint. Details such as sleep, appetite, preferences for heat or cold, and how you respond to stress are part of how a homoeopath chooses a medicine.

How often will I need a follow-up?

It varies from person to person. At the end of each visit the doctor will tell you when to come back. Usual interval: [follow-up interval]

What if I feel unwell between visits?

Contact the clinic if you have a question about your medicines. If symptoms are severe or getting worse quickly, see a doctor or go to the nearest hospital straight away. In an emergency, call 112.
