How Dr. Sachdeva works

Unhurried, individual and clear

Classical homoeopathy looks at the whole person rather than a single complaint. That takes time, so every consultation is built around three simple ideas.

01

A detailed history

Your first visit covers your present concerns, past illnesses, family history, sleep, appetite, energy and temperament — the details a homoeopath uses when choosing a medicine.

02

An individual prescription

Medicines are selected for you as an individual, with clear instructions on how and when to take them and what to notice between visits.

03

Steady follow-up

Each follow-up reviews how you have been since the last visit. The doctor adjusts the plan where needed and tells you when to return.

Your first visit

What happens at a first consultation

No preparation is required, but a little helps. Here is the shape of a first visit.

Read the first-consultation guide

  1. Get in touch

    Write or call to confirm timings and a suitable time to visit.

  2. Bring your papers

    Previous reports, prescriptions and a list of the medicines you take.

  3. The conversation

    A detailed, unhurried history. Some questions may seem unrelated; that is normal.

  4. Your plan

    Medicines, clear instructions and when to come back for a follow-up.
