Gynaecology & obstetrics

Pregnancy & antenatal care

From confirming a pregnancy to planning for birth, regular check-ups keep track of your health and your baby’s growth, with time for your questions at every visit.

Read the pregnancy guide

Often includes

  • Check-ups through each trimester
  • Routine tests and scans, as advised
  • Guidance on diet, supplements and activity
  • Planning for birth and recovery

Health conditions in pregnancy

Closer monitoring and care when a health concern comes up before or during pregnancy, such as changes in blood pressure or blood sugar.

Often includes

  • Extra check-ups where needed
  • Tests to keep an eye on the condition
  • Clear guidance on warning signs

Gynaecological concerns

A private consultation for period problems, discomfort or any other women’s health concern, with a clear explanation of what may be going on and the options.

Often includes

  • A detailed history and examination
  • Tests or scans, if needed
  • A treatment plan explained in plain language

Contraception & family planning

Advice on contraceptive options that suit your health and stage of life, and help planning a pregnancy when you are ready.

Often includes

  • A discussion of contraceptive options
  • Pre-pregnancy check-up and advice
  • Contraception after childbirth

Menopause guidance

Support for the physical and emotional changes around menopause, and advice on staying well through it.

Often includes

  • A discussion of symptoms and concerns
  • Lifestyle and treatment options
  • Routine health check-ups

Fertility consultations

If you have been trying to conceive, a consultation can look at possible reasons and talk through next steps, at a pace that feels right.

Often includes

  • History and evaluation, as advised
  • Tests and scans, if needed
  • A clear plan of next steps

Uterine fibroids

Evaluation of fibroids and a discussion of the options, from monitoring to treatment, based on your symptoms and plans.

Often includes

  • Ultrasound and other tests, as advised
  • Options explained clearly
  • Follow-up visits
