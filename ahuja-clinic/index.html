    First trimester

    Weeks 1–13

  2. 02

    Second trimester

    Weeks 14–27

    Routine checks, the detailed anomaly scan and a blood sugar test, as advised.

  3. 03

    Third trimester

    Weeks 28–40

    More frequent visits, your baby’s growth and position, and planning for birth.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Which doctor should I see?

For pregnancy, periods, contraception, menopause or any other women’s health concern, book with Dr. Ruchi Rai Ahuja (gynaecologist & obstetrician). For general surgical concerns, book with Dr. Alok Ahuja (general surgeon). Not sure? Mention it in your enquiry and the clinic can guide you.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Each doctor’s consultation hours may differ, so please confirm your slot when you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call or send an enquiry before you visit so a time can be confirmed. [walk-in policy]

Where exactly is the clinic?

No. 109, Sector 18-A, near the Rotary Club, Chandigarh. The contact page has a map and directions.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous prescriptions, test results and scan reports, and a list of the medicines you take. For a pregnancy visit, note the first day of your last period.

Is the enquiry form private?

The form on this website stores nothing. It simply opens a pre-filled message in your own email app (or WhatsApp), which you can check before sending.

Questions before you visit?

Send a short enquiry. It opens in your email app, ready to send.
