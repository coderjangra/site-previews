Services for adults and children

Everything Kalia Dental lists, from prevention to replacing missing teeth. Each one links to a plain-language guide with the usual number of visits and aftercare tips.

01

Check-ups & prevention

Finding problems early, and keeping teeth and gums healthy between visits.

Digital check-ups

An examination of your teeth, gums and bite. A sensible first step if you are unsure what you need.

Visits & aftercare

Digital X-rays

Images of what cannot be seen in the mouth, such as roots, bone and the spaces between teeth.

Visits & aftercare

Teeth cleaning

Ultrasonic scaling followed by polishing, to remove tartar and surface stains that brushing leaves behind.

Visits & aftercare

Fluoride treatment

A fluoride gel or varnish applied to the teeth to help strengthen enamel against decay.

Visits & aftercare
02

Repairing teeth

Treating decay, infection and damage while keeping as much of your own tooth as possible.

Tooth-coloured fillings

Composite resin fillings matched to your tooth shade, used to fill cavities.

Visits & aftercare

Root canal treatment

Clears infected pulp from inside the tooth and seals the canals, usually followed by a crown.

Visits & aftercare

Crowns

A cap that covers and protects a damaged or weakened tooth.

Visits & aftercare
03

Replacing missing teeth

Options for filling a gap, discussed after an examination and X-rays.

Dental implants

A titanium or zirconia post in place of a missing root, topped with a crown, bridge or denture.

Visits & aftercare

Bridges

An artificial tooth held in place by the teeth on either side of the gap.

Visits & aftercare
04

Oral surgery

When a tooth cannot be saved.

Tooth extraction

Removal of a tooth under local anaesthetic, with clear aftercare instructions for healing at home.

Visits & aftercare
05

Children

Good habits and early checks from the first teeth onwards.

Children's dentistry

Child-friendly check-ups and preventive care, plus brushing and diet advice for parents.

Tips for parents
06

Smile appearance

Changes to how teeth look, planned around healthy teeth and gums.

Cosmetic dentistry

Treatments that change the shade or shape of teeth. Which options suit you is discussed at a consultation.

Visits & aftercare

Severe pain or swelling? Call the clinic on +91 81099 37835. If swelling affects your breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Not sure which treatment you need?

Start with a check-up. Dr. Kalia will examine your teeth and explain the options.
