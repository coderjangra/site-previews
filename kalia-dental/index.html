Do you see children?

Yes. Children's (paediatric) dentistry is one of the services the clinic lists, with a focus on check-ups and preventive care. See the tips for parents.

How many visits does a root canal take?

Often one to three visits, depending on the tooth and how much infection there is. A crown is often advised afterwards to protect the tooth. Dr. Kalia will confirm after examining it.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any earlier dental X-rays or reports, and a list of the medicines you take and any health conditions you have. These help the dentist plan treatment safely.

How much will my treatment cost?

Costs depend on what you need, which is only clear after an examination. Please ask the clinic directly.
