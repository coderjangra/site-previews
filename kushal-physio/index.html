Why at home

Care planned around the spaces you actually move in

At home, the physiotherapist can see the bed you get up from, the chair you sit in and the steps you climb, and shape exercises around them.

  • No travel, parking or waiting room for the patient
  • Exercises planned around your own furniture and space
  • Family members or carers can be shown how to help safely
  • Sessions in English, Hindi or Punjabi

How a home visit works

From first message to first session

  1. Message or call

    Share the patient’s concern, your area and a time that suits you.

  2. Fix a visit

    A short call to understand the situation and agree a date and time.

  3. Assessment at home

    History, reports and a look at how the patient sits, stands and walks.

  4. Plan & sessions

    A treatment and home-exercise plan, reviewed as sessions go on.

Good to know before you book

Ask on WhatsApp
Which areas do you cover for home visits?

Home visits are listed for Zirakpur, Chandigarh, Mohali, Dhakoli, Peer Muchala and Derabassi. If you live nearby but outside these areas, send a message and ask.

Do I need a doctor’s prescription?

You can get in touch directly. If the patient has a prescription, discharge summary, X-rays or scan reports, please keep them ready, as they help with the assessment.

What happens on the first visit?

The first visit is mainly an assessment: a conversation about the concern and medical history, a look at any reports, and simple checks of how the patient moves. You then discuss a plan and how often sessions may be needed.

How much does a session cost?

Fees depend on the type of consultation (home, clinic or online). Please call or WhatsApp for the current charges.

Can sessions be done online?

Tele-physiotherapy is offered for consultations, exercise guidance and follow-ups. Whether it suits the patient is decided after a conversation.

Is this service for emergencies?

No. For a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.
