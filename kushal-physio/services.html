Areas of care

Where physiotherapy can help

Physiotherapy supports movement, strength and everyday function. These are areas people commonly ask about; whether physiotherapy is suitable is decided after an assessment. [confirm the list of services you offer]

MPT (Neurology)

Neurological physiotherapy

Support with movement, balance and daily tasks for people living with conditions such as stroke, Parkinson’s disease or nerve injury.

Back & neck pain

Exercise, posture and movement advice for back and neck discomfort, including strain from long hours at a desk.

Joint pain & stiffness

Guided exercise and mobility work for stiff or painful knees, shoulders and hips.

After surgery or a fracture

Rehabilitation after joint replacement, a fracture or other surgery, in line with your surgeon’s advice.

Older adults & balance

Strength, walking and balance exercises, with practical advice on moving safely around the home.

Limited mobility at home

Gentle movement, positioning and breathing exercises for patients who spend much of the day in bed, with guidance for carers.

Physiotherapy is one part of care and works alongside your doctor’s advice. Progress differs from person to person, and no particular result can be promised.

What a session involves

What to expect

  1. Assessment

    A conversation about the concern and history, a look at reports, and checks of movement, strength and balance.

  2. Treatment

    Guided exercise and hands-on techniques, chosen for the patient and explained as you go.

  3. Home programme

    Simple exercises to keep up between sessions, set up around your own space.

  4. Review

    The plan is checked and adjusted over the following sessions.
