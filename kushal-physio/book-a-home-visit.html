How it works

How a home visit works

Four simple steps, from your first message to regular sessions.

  1. Send the details

    Use the form on this page, WhatsApp or a call. A few lines about the patient, your area and a preferred time are enough.

  2. Agree a time

    A short conversation to understand the situation and fix a date and time for the first visit.

  3. First visit: assessment

    Medical history, any reports, and checks of how the patient moves, sits, stands and walks at home.

  4. Plan and sessions

    A plan of treatment and home exercises is discussed, along with how often sessions may be needed.

Conditions commonly seen

What people often ask for help with

Described in general terms. Your physiotherapist will assess whether physiotherapy is suitable, and may suggest seeing a doctor.

  • Back and neck pain
  • Knee, shoulder or hip stiffness
  • Recovery after surgery or a fracture
  • After a stroke
  • Parkinson’s and other neurological conditions
  • Balance and walking difficulties
  • Weakness after illness or a hospital stay
  • Limited mobility or mostly in bed

Before the visit

  • Keep prescriptions, discharge summaries and scan reports handy
  • The patient can wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Clear a little floor space and keep a firm chair nearby
  • A family member or carer is welcome to be there

Home-visit questions

Before you book

Prefer to talk it through? Call and ask.

Call +91 82220 22138
Which areas do you cover for home visits?

Home visits are listed for Zirakpur, Chandigarh, Mohali, Dhakoli, Peer Muchala and Derabassi. If you live nearby but outside these areas, send a message and ask.

Do I need a doctor’s prescription?

You can get in touch directly. If the patient has a prescription, discharge summary, X-rays or scan reports, please keep them ready, as they help with the assessment.

What happens on the first visit?

The first visit is mainly an assessment: a conversation about the concern and medical history, a look at any reports, and simple checks of how the patient moves. You then discuss a plan and how often sessions may be needed.

How much does a session cost?

Fees depend on the type of consultation (home, clinic or online). Please call or WhatsApp for the current charges.

Is this service for emergencies?

No. For a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.
