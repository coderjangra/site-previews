Listed services

What Shiv Home Relocation India offers.

Service 01

Household shifting

Packing, loading, transport and unloading of household goods, from a 1RK to a 3BHK or larger home.

Listed service

Good to share for a quote

  • Home size (1RK, 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK+)
  • Floor and lift access at both addresses
  • Heavy items: fridge, almirah, sofa, washing machine
  • Fragile items such as glass, TV or a dressing mirror

Service 02

Car carrier service

Your car transported on a carrier, booked together with your household shifting or on its own.

Listed service

Good to share for a quote

  • Car make and model
  • Pickup and drop cities
  • Preferred pickup date
  • Whether it moves with the household goods

Service 03

Warehousing

Storage for household goods when your new home is not ready, or during a gap between moves.

Listed service

Good to share for a quote

  • Roughly how much is to be stored (or the home size)
  • For how long
  • Whether you will need access in between
  • Where the goods go after storage

Service 04

Commodity insurance

Insurance for goods is listed among the services. Cover details are not published here, so ask the team.

Listed service

Ask the team about

  • Which cover options are available
  • How the value of goods is declared
  • What the terms and premium are
  • How a claim would be raised
