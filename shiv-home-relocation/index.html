How do I get a price for my move?

Open the moving quote page and add your route, home size, move date and whether a car or bike is coming along. It turns your answers into a ready-to-send message for Shiv Home Relocation India, and the team replies with a quote.

Can my car move along with the household goods?

Car carrier service is one of the listed services. Mention the vehicle in the quote form so it is planned together with the rest of the move.

Do you offer storage between moves?

Warehousing is listed among the services. Ask about the storage period and terms when you request your quote.

Is insurance available for my goods?

Commodity insurance is listed as a service. Ask the team about cover options and terms before you book.
