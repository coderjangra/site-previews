The process

From first message to first day.

You stay in charge at every step. You choose who joins your home, and nothing is fixed until you agree the duties, timings and start date.

Skip to the request form

  1. Step 1

    Tell us your need

    Share who needs care, the duties, the shift, your area and when you would like someone to start. A WhatsApp message or a call to +91 95011 32949 is enough, and the form below can write the message for you.

  2. Step 2

    Shortlist

    We look at your requirement and suggest candidates who match it: the role, the shift, the area and anything else you have told us.

  3. Step 3

    Meet and interview

    Speak to or meet the shortlisted candidates. Ask about their experience and routine, and choose the person who feels right for your home.

  4. Step 4

    Verification

    Before joining, go through the candidate’s ID and reference details. [confirm what the agency verifies]

  5. Step 5

    Joining

    Agree the duties, timings and start date. The caretaker joins on the day you have fixed, and you can reach us if anything needs discussing.

  • The hours and days you need help

  • Any duties beyond care, like cooking
  • Your preferred start date
  • A budget range you have in mind
